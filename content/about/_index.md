Peak Contract Flooring, LLC is a commercial and residential flooring contractor
based in Atlanta, GA, led by Matt. We do one thing — install floors — and we do
it with our own crew, not subs we've never met.

## How we work

Every job starts with a walkthrough and a written estimate: materials, labor,
prep, and timeline, with no surprises added later. We check subfloors and
moisture before we quote, because that's where flooring problems actually come
from. And we finish with a walkthrough — the job isn't done until you've looked
at every transition and corner with us.
