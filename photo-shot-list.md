# Photo Shot List

The site lives or dies on photos. This is everything needed before launch,
in priority order. Phone camera is fine — shoot horizontal, in daylight or
with all the lights on, and wipe the floor first.

## Must-have (blocks launch)

1. **Hero shot (1)** — the single best finished floor he's ever done, wide
   angle, ideally with natural light raking across it. This is the homepage.
2. **One feature photo per project (4+)** — the "cover" for each gallery
   entry. Wide shot showing the whole room/space.
3. **Service photos (4)** — one representative photo each for commercial,
   hardwood, LVP, and tile/carpet pages. Can reuse project photos.

## Should-have

4. **6–10 photos per project** — mix of: wide room shot, medium shot from a
   doorway, close-up of the material/grain, one detail shot (transition,
   stair nose, tight cut around a doorframe — the details are what sell
   craftsmanship to people who know).
5. **Before/after pairs** — shoot the "before" on the next few jobs going
   forward. Same angle for both shots. These convert better than anything.
6. **Owner/crew photo (1)** — on a job site, not a studio. For the About page.

## File conventions

- Folder per project: `static/images/work/<project-slug>/`
- Name the cover `feature.jpg`, the rest `01.jpg`, `02.jpg`, ...
- Resize to max 2000px wide before uploading (Hugo will generate smaller
  sizes, but don't feed it 12MB originals)
- **Use JPG.** (If he ever switches to the gallery theme, WebP has a known
  Hugo bug; JPG avoids it everywhere.)

## Going forward

End-of-job habit: 10 minutes of photos after final cleanup, before furniture
goes back. That habit is the whole marketing engine for this site.
